Tips

🧃 Your drinks
🍹Have the mid-morning juice fresh, about 200 ml. The vitamin C from amla or orange helps your body absorb iron from plant foods.
🌙Have the bedtime drink about 2 hours after dinner. Milk's calcium can block iron absorption, so keep it away from meals.
🩸For low haemoglobin, ask your doctor for a CBC, ferritin and B12 test. Don't take iron tablets without testing.
✨ Daily habits
🌱Add 1 tbsp ground flaxseed each day to yogurt, milk, chilla batter or dal.
🥕Eat 2–3 coloured vegetables a day: greens plus orange or red ones.
🍚Swap white rice for brown rice, millet or quinoa when you can.
☕Skip tea and coffee within an hour of lunch and dinner. It reduces iron absorption.
🫒Use moderate oil or ghee, go easy on salt, and keep sugar and fried snacks low.
🧴Wear sunscreen daily, sleep 7–8 hours, and move every day.
🔁Skin renews over about 4 weeks, so repeat the plan for lasting results.
🔪 Prep ahead
🫘Soak rajma and chole the night before, or use canned.
🌿Start sprouts two days early. Soak whole moong overnight, then keep it in a damp cloth.
🍇Soak 8–10 raisins overnight for the next morning's snack.
🥞Keep dosa batter in the fridge for quick uttapam and ragi dosa breakfasts. Mix ragi flour into it for ragi dosa.
🥛Make buttermilk by blending yogurt with water, a pinch of salt and roasted cumin. It's a great extra drink on hot days.
